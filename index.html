WELCOME TO THE GARDEN

HOW TO PLAY

A few things to know before your first run.

1
Move & Dodge

Tap or drag to move your bug. Keep moving when enemies close in.

2
Attacks Are Automatic

Your bug automatically attacks the closest enemy, so focus on positioning and staying alive.

3
Collect & Save Leaves

Leaves carry over between runs. Pick them up and save them — you will need a lot of them.

4
Permanent Upgrades Matter

Spend saved Leaves on Permanent Upgrades between runs. They are a big part of getting farther.

You can read the full How to Play guide anytime in Settings.
